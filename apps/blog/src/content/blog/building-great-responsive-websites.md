Foreword: I actively help new — and not so new — developers learn programming, and one of the most constant struggles they face is building great responsive websites. The approach is usually building the site for desktop, and then realizing on resizing the browser, or opening it on their phone, that this doesn’t work. Then the plan becomes trying to force this website to display properly, and realizing they don’t really understand the how. Usually, the attempts will force elements a specific size, or worse, force moving them specific places.

## First not-so-good approach

Before understanding the proper ways, I believe it’s important to understand the not-so-good approaches, and why they don’t work well.

Let’s start with the initial implementation, we’ll be covering the various approaches I see in the wild to even attempt desktop layouts.
